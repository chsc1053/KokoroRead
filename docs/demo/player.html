Reading without giving your words away

KokoroRead · on-device speech · WebGPU

Kokoro is a Japanese word that translates to “heart” or “spirit.” KokoroRead keeps that spirit: your articles stay in the browser while a compact neural voice reads them aloud.

Select a passage or open the floating player to read the full page. Pause, skip chunks, and nudge the speaking rate — all without a cloud TTS API.

After the first model cache, synthesis continues offline. System voices remain available when you want instant playback without downloading weights.
